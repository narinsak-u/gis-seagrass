# Seagrass Monitoring

Terms for monitored seagrass areas in this system.

## Language

**Site**:
A monitored seagrass area identified by a code and geographic boundary.

**Soft-deleted site**:
A site retained as a historical record but no longer active. Its code remains reserved.

**Survey**:
A dated observation of seagrass cover linked to a Site and located at a geographic point.
